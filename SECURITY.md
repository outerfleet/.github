# Security policy

This policy covers every OuterFleet repository that does not have its own `SECURITY.md`.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please do not open a public issue. Report it privately through GitHub's
**Report a vulnerability** form on the affected repository, under the **Security** tab.

Include what you did, what happened, and what you expected, with steps to reproduce if you can.

## What to expect

OuterFleet is a small team. We read every report, confirm what we can reproduce, and fix
confirmed issues before discussing them publicly. Please give us reasonable time to ship a fix
before you disclose.
